Quick survival guide:
Mean = total ÷ count
Median = middle of ordered values
Mode = most frequent value
Range = maximum − minimum
Population variance = average squared distance from mean (divide by N)
Standard deviation = √variance

Variance questions use population variance, not sample variance.

🎓 Your take-home cheat sheet

Central tendency tells us about a typical value: mean, median, mode.

Variation tells us how spread out values are: range, variance, standard deviation.

Population variance divides by N. Sample variance usually divides by n − 1. Standard deviation uses the original units.
